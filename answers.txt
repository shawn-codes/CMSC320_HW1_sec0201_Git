
Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git is useful because it lets you keep track of changes made to a project over time. 
It also makes it easier for multiple people to work on the same project, compare changes, and go back to an earlier version if a mistake is made. 
This helps keep projects organized and makes collaboration much smoother.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science can be used in a wide range of fields, such as healthcare, finance, education, and sports. 
For example, in healthcare, doctors and researchers can use data science to analyze patient information and identify patterns 
that may help detect diseases earlier and improve treatment plans.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging combines two branches by creating a merge commit and preserving the complete history of both branches, 
while rebasing moves a branch's commits onto another branch to create a cleaner, more linear history by rewriting the commit history.
